# Ticket & Hospitality Research: FC Barcelona vs. Celta de Vigo
**Date:** December 6, 2026 (La Liga - Spotify Camp Nou)

Here is a detailed research summary of the ticket and hospitality options for the FC Barcelona vs. Celta de Vigo fixture at the Spotify Camp Nou:

---

### 1. Official Club Standard & Package Tickets (FC Barcelona Official Channel / Authorized Agents)
- **Overview:** Purchased directly via FC Barcelona's official channels or long-standing authorized agents (e.g., official ticketing partners like Barcelona.com or P1 Travel).
- **Pricing & Availability:** Standard tickets / official packages typically start around **€180 – €239+ per person**.
- **What's Included:** Guaranteed entry, side-by-side seating for pairs, official match programme or Barça Museum fast-track entry packages.
- **Pros & Cons:** 100% secure with zero risk of gate rejection. Exact seat numbers within the chosen category are assigned closer to matchday.

### 2. Secondary Marketplaces (StubHub / Viagogo)
- **Overview:** Resale platforms where season ticket holders or brokers list their seats.
- **Pricing & Availability:** General admission / behind-the-goal (Gol 2 / Gol 1) listings start around **€149 – €165 per ticket**, while corner and tier-2 seats range from **€170 – €200+ per ticket**.
- **What's Included:** Digital or electronic ticket transfers with standard platform buyer guarantees.
- **Pros & Cons:** Good flexibility for finding specific sections. However, prices fluctuate based on demand, and tickets are often delivered only 24–48 hours before kick-off.

### 3. Authorized VIP Hospitality Packages (DN Global / Official Hospitality Agents)
- **Overview:** Premium hospitality packages sourced through authorized corporate hospitality suppliers.
- **Pricing & Availability:** All-inclusive hospitality packages start around **£335 / €390+ per person**.
- **What's Included:** Premium padded match seats in prime stadium locations, exclusive pre-match and half-time VIP lounge access, gourmet catering/buffet, complimentary bar, and dedicated VIP stadium entrances.
- **Pros & Cons:** Excellent for a luxury or bucket-list experience with zero secondary-market risk and all-inclusive pricing (no hidden booking fees).
