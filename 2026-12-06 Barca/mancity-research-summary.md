# Ticket & Hospitality Research: Manchester City vs. Chelsea
**Date:** December 12, 2026 17:30 GMT (Premier League - Etihad Stadium)

Here is a detailed research summary of the ticket and hospitality options for the Manchester City vs. Chelsea fixture at the Etihad Stadium:

---

### 1. Official Ticket & Hotel Breaks (SportsBreaks / Official Club Suppliers)
- **Overview:** Official match breaks partnered with Manchester City FC, combining tickets with local hotel accommodations in Manchester.
- **Pricing & Availability:** Starting around **£199 per person** for General Admission / Tier 3 East Stand packages including an overnight hotel stay with breakfast.
- **What's Included:** Official match ticket, 1-night hotel stay in Manchester, and optional add-ons like Uber vouchers or stadium tours.
- **Pros & Cons:** Ideal for travelers needing both lodging and guaranteed tickets in one seamless official booking.

### 2. Mid-Tier Lounge Hospitality (93:20 Lounge / Kits Sports Bar / Citizens Hospitality)
- **Overview:** Popular Etihad Stadium hospitality lounges offering vibrant pre- and post-match atmospheres with executive middle-tier seating.
- **Pricing & Availability:** Ranging from **£229 – £298 per person** (via providers like SportsBreaks, Champions Travel, and DN Global).
- **What's Included:** Executive middle-tier padded seats (Blocks 109/110/121 or Level 1 halfway line), access to the lounge 1.5–3 hours pre-match and 1 hour post-match, complimentary match program, food options to purchase or included 3-course buffet, and welcome drinks/beer tokens.
- **Pros & Cons:** Great value-for-money upgrade combining lively fan culture with comfortable seating and lounge amenities.

### 3. Elite VIP Hospitality (The Green Room & Tunnel Club Premier)
- **Overview:** The pinnacle of Etihad Stadium matchday experiences, featuring silver-service fine dining or direct player tunnel views.
- **Pricing & Availability:** Premium tier ranging from **£400 to £750+ per person**.
- **What's Included:** Private balcony seating (Zadie's Balcony in the North Stand) or heated VIP seats directly behind the dugouts, silver-service pre-match fine dining, premium complimentary bars (spirits, wines, prosecco), and pitchside warm-up access or player arrival views.
- **Pros & Cons:** The ultimate luxury matchday experience, though significantly higher in cost.
